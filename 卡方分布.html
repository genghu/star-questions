1 计算概率 P(4 <= chi^2 <= 9)

Assume chi^2 follows a chi-square distribution with 7 degrees of freedom. Calculate P(4 <= chi^2 <= 9).

Round your answer to at least three decimal places.

Hint: P(4 <= chi^2 <= 9) = P(chi^2 > 4) - P(chi^2 > 9)

Correct Answer: 0.527
Key Points and Analysis

According to the problem, P(4 <= chi^2 <= 9) = P(chi^2 > 4) - P(chi^2 > 9).

Using a calculator: P(chi^2 > 4) = 0.77977741, P(chi^2 > 9) = 0.25265605.

Therefore, P(4 <= chi^2 <= 9) = 0.77977741 - 0.25265605 ~= 0.527.

So the correct answer is 0.527.

2 Find k such that P(chi^2 > k) = 0.025

Again assume chi^2 follows a chi-square distribution with 7 degrees of freedom. Find k such that P(chi^2 > k) = 0.025.

Round your answer to at least two decimal places.

Hint: Such k is the right-tail critical value of the chi-square distribution chi^2_0.025.

Correct Answer: 16.01
Key Points and Analysis

We need to find the right-tail critical value for a chi-square distribution with 7 degrees of freedom, such that the right-tail area is 0.025.

According to the problem, using a calculator: k = chi^2_0.025 = 16.01276427 ~= 16.01.

So the correct answer is 16.01.

3 Find the Median of Chi-Square Distribution

Find the median of a chi-square distribution with 7 degrees of freedom.

Round your answer to at least two decimal places.

Hint: The median m satisfies P(chi^2 > m) = 0.5, i.e., chi^2_0.5.

Correct Answer: 6.35
Key Points and Analysis

The median of a continuous distribution is the value that divides the distribution in half, i.e., the probability of obtaining a value greater than the median is 0.5.

Therefore, we need to find m such that P(chi^2 > m) = 0.5, where chi^2 follows a chi-square distribution with 7 degrees of freedom.

According to the problem, using a calculator: m = chi^2_0.5 ~= 6.3458112 ~= 6.35.

So the correct answer is 6.35.
